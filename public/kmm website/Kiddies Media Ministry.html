Who we are

The framework that children's media works within

KMM provides the governance, policy, and standards framework for children's content across Loveworld Kingdom.

Our purpose is to guide, coordinate, and set expectations for children's media initiatives, so that teams across the ministry can create and share content with clarity and confidence.

What KMM doesGuides, coordinates and establishes the expectations children's media initiatives follow.
What KMM is notA studio that simply produces entertainment content.

Our mandate

Eight areas of responsibility

From policy to compliance, these are the areas KMM covers on behalf of children's media across the ministry.

Governance and Policy

Establish and maintain policies, governance frameworks, and operating guidelines for children's content.

Content Standards

Define and communicate expectations for content quality, suitability, consistency, and alignment with ministry values.

Child Protection and Responsible Media

Promote age-appropriate content, safeguarding principles, responsible representation of children, privacy, and ethical media practices.

Intellectual Property and Copyright

Provide guidance on content ownership, copyright compliance, permissions, licensing, and appropriate use of creative assets.

AI and Digital Media Governance

Guide the responsible use of AI-generated content, digital tools, emerging technologies, and related creative workflows.

Governance & Legal Accreditation

Institutional training and regulatory qualification for ministry legal counsels, child safeguarding auditors, and broadcast compliance reviewers.

Collaboration and Coordination

Facilitate structured collaboration, accountability, and alignment among relevant children's media teams and initiatives.

Monitoring and Compliance

Support the implementation, review, and continuous improvement of established policies and standards.

These are KMM's proposed public-facing areas of responsibility, subject to confirmation against officially approved policies and organizational mandates.

KMM Governance, Legal & Policy Academy

Institutional accreditation & compliance portal

The academy is the official institutional learning environment dedicated exclusively to Governance, Legal Jurisprudence, Statutory Child Protection, and Intellectual Property Policy. Ministry legal counsels, safeguarding auditors, and broadcast compliance reviewers complete mandatory accreditation to enforce zero-defect standards across all Loveworld media channels.
